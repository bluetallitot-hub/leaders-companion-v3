1. Original source files

Choose the two files to calculate the intake inventory.

2. Page selection method

Both methods select bilingual facing pairs. Every Hebrew page must have its matching English page and vice versa. Missing counterparts require review; no page is silently substituted.

Upload a CSV to estimate the selected printed pages.

3. Highlight roles and opacity

Colors and roles are read from the cue sheet. Choose which imported role belongs to each color, then adjust opacity against the writing sample. Up to six colors are supported. The colors themselves cannot be changed.

Upload a cue CSV to load its roles.

The new PDF preserves the original pages and embeds the edited CSV as a PDF attachment. Highlighting and page mapping are not yet applied by intake.

Set opacity before starting page editing. Once fixed, it applies throughout the siddur.
